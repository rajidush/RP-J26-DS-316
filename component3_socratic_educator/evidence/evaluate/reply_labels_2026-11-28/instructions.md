# Reply-label sheet: instructions

Each row is one child reply. Read `dialogue_so_far`, then label `child_reply` only, on three parts. Type one value from each list below. To leave a cell out, type `skip`: it is recorded as missing, never as a judgement. Rows marked `sensitive` = yes come from self-harm or sexual-content situations; skip them whenever you prefer. Use `notes` for anything else. Do not change any other column, and save the file as CSV UTF-8.

## `expressed_emotion`

The feeling the reply conveys, named or not (GoEmotions' Ekman grouping).

- `anger`: anger, annoyance, disapproval
- `disgust`: disgust, being grossed out
- `fear`: fear, nervousness, worry
- `joy`: joy, amusement, relief, excitement, gratitude, pride, optimism
- `sadness`: sadness, disappointment, embarrassment, grief, remorse
- `surprise`: surprise, confusion, realisation, curiosity
- `neutral`: no feeling conveyed

## `distress_severity`

How much distress the reply shows (after the ReachOut triage scheme).

- `none`: no distress
- `past`: distress the child says is over ("it scared me then, I'm okay now")
- `current_mild`: distress now, softened or manageable ("kinda scary", "a bit worried")
- `current_acute`: strong distress now, being overwhelmed, any self-harm language, or the child threatening or saying they want to hurt someone (not a denial such as "I would never hurt anyone", not someone else's harm, not play in a game)

## `stance`

How the reply relates to taking a safer step (Motivational Interviewing client language).

- `change_talk`: the child moves toward a safer step: wants to, can, or will take one
- `neutral`: answers or describes without leaning toward or away from a safer step
- `sustain_talk`: argues for the risky behaviour or plays it down ("it's just a joke")
- `discord`: friction with the educator ("stop asking me", "it's not my fault" said at the educator)
- `disengaged`: non-answers and opting out ("idk", "nothing", "leave me alone"); a project code, not MI
